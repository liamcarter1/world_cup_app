--------------------------------------------------------
THE IDEA
--------------------------------------------------------
Each of us has been given 8 World Cup teams (picked at
random - 48 teams shared between the 6 of us).

The further your teams go in the tournament, the more
points you score. Last family member standing wins!

--------------------------------------------------------
THE FOUR BUTTONS ALONG THE TOP
--------------------------------------------------------
   HOME      - The leaderboard (who's winning) and the
               prize pot.

   FAMILY    - Tap your name to see your 8 teams, how
               they're doing, and when they play next.

   BRACKET   - The knockout chart, coloured by who owns
               each team, so you can see whose teams are
               still alive.

   FIXTURES  - Every match, with kick-off times and
               scores.


--------------------------------------------------------
SCORES
--------------------------------------------------------
Scores update on their own - just reopen the app or pull
down to refresh. A red "LIVE" tag means a match is being
played right now.


--------------------------------------------------------
HOW YOU SCORE POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------
Your teams earn points each time they reach the next
round:
   Group stage -> Last 32 -> Last 16 -> Quarter-finals
   -> Semi-finals -> Final

The more of your teams that go far, the higher you climb
the leaderboard.


--------------------------------------------------------
THE PRIZE POT  (5 pounds each = 30 pounds total)
--------------------------------------------------------
   1st - 20 pounds : whoever owns the team that WINS
                     the World Cup
   2nd -  7 pounds : whoever owns the RUNNER-UP
   3rd -  3 pounds : whoever's teams score the MOST GOALS
